Questions, answered

A little more clarity.

Who can apply?

This prototype is designed for residents of the United States and Canada. Production eligibility requirements will be configured by the lender.

Do you offer personal loans?

The prototype supports personal loan and business financing application paths.

What information is required?

Applicants may be asked for contact, address, employment or business, income, and purpose information.

What documents may be required?

Documents may be requested to verify identity, income, or business information. Do not upload real documents to this prototype.

How long does review take?

Review timing varies based on the application and information required.

Does applying guarantee approval?

No. Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or a commitment to lend.

Will my credit be checked?

Any credit-report authorization must be explained separately and handled under the applicable legal and compliance process.

How is my information protected?

This static demo does not transmit or store application data. Production use requires secure authenticated infrastructure.

What happens after I submit?

You receive an application reference and further instructions through the appropriate communication channel.

How will I receive a decision?

The production workflow will specify approved communication channels for status updates and notices.
